# Raahi pitch outline

1. **Title:** Raahi — offline mountain decision intelligence
2. **Problem:** maps persist offline, current trail evidence does not
3. **User:** occasional travellers on unfamiliar one-day mountain hikes
4. **Insight:** information confidence is different from route safety
5. **Solution:** journey packs, Decision Checkpoints, community breadcrumbs and Trust Receipts
6. **Exasol:** primary store and analytical engine for rules, corroboration, freshness and segment conditions
7. **AI:** converts unstructured observations into validated hazard records; structured fallback remains available
8. **Demo:** offline report → reconnect → Exasol recalculation → improved next journey pack
9. **Responsible design:** uncertainty, expiry, source hierarchy, no safety guarantees
10. **Roadmap:** verified guides, official feeds, multiple trails and low-bandwidth weather updates
